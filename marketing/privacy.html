Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

At Styld, we build tools that help beauty and wellness professionals run and grow their businesses. We are proud to support stylists, barbers, makeup artists, nail technicians, lash artists, and related professionals (“Professionals”) with booking, client management, websites, communications, and payment technology (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains how MOUS APPS LLC (“MOUS APPS LLC,” “Styld,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you use styldd.com, tenant sites such as {yourname}.styldd.com, the Styld mobile application (the “App”), and related products and support. Styld is a trade name and brand of MOUS APPS LLC.

Who This Policy Applies To

Professionals (business users)

This Policy describes our practices when you register for a Styld account, subscribe, publish a site, manage clients, or otherwise use the Services as a Professional or Account Owner.

End Clients (consumers who book or buy)

If you book an appointment, purchase a product, pay a deposit, upload photos, leave a review, or otherwise interact with a Professional through a Styld-powered booking page or site, two parties may process your information:

  1. The Professional — generally decides why and how your information is used for the appointment or purchase (they are often the “business” or “controller” under privacy laws).
  2. Styld — hosts the technology, processes bookings and payments, sends operational messages, and uses information as described in this Policy (often as a “service provider” / “processor” on the Professional’s behalf, and as a controller for our own operational and product purposes).

If you have questions about how a specific Professional uses your data, contact that Professional directly and review any privacy notice they provide on their site. This Policy explains Styld’s practices.

Summary

  • We collect information you provide, information collected automatically, and information from service providers (e.g., payment processors).
  • We use information to operate the Services, process bookings and payments, communicate with you, improve our products, and comply with law.
  • We share information with vendors, payment partners, and others as described below—not for selling SMS opt-in data.
  • Professionals are responsible for their own client lists, marketing consents, and compliance when they use Styld to contact clients.
  • You may have rights to access, delete, or correct personal information depending on where you live.

Professionals: You are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to upload client data and to send marketing or reminder messages through Styld, including obtaining required consents under TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and similar laws.

We and our partners may use cookies, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies on our marketing site and, where applicable, logged-in web experiences to:

Depending on your jurisdiction, some of these activities may be considered “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising” under state privacy laws.

Your choices:

  • Opt out of promotional emails via unsubscribe links;
  • Limit ad tracking in iOS/Android device settings.

When we publish a live privacy rights page at https://styldd.com/privacy, we will provide a “Your Privacy Rights” link for cookie and targeted advertising opt-outs consistent with applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, and comply with legal obligations (tax, payment records, dispute resolution). Retention periods vary by data type—for example, active account data is kept while your account is open; backup copies may persist for a limited period after deletion.

When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify personal information except where retention is required by law or legitimate business needs (e.g., fraud prevention, chargeback records maintained by payment partners).

Professionals: Client data in your account may be deleted when you delete your account; export data before deletion if you need a copy.
