Getting started

Styld is a mobile CRM app and booking website platform for independent beauty professionals. You run your business from the iOS app — calendar, clients, services, payments — and Styld publishes a branded booking site at yourname.styldd.com where clients book and pay online.

Styld is built for independent beauty pros: hairstylists & braiders, barbers, makeup artists, nail techs, and lash techs. If you book clients and want to ditch the DMs, Styld was made for you.

DMs are a black hole — missed messages, no deposit tracking, no calendar. Styld gives you a real booking link, automated confirmations, online payments, and a client history you can actually use. Your clients book themselves while you focus on the work.

Styld is available on the iOS App Store. Search "Styld" or tap the download button on this page. Android is coming — stay tuned.

Download the Styld app → set up your business profile → add your services and hours → customize your booking site → subscribe → publish. Your site goes live at yourname.styldd.com and clients can book immediately.

There's no free tier that keeps your booking site live. A subscription is required to publish and maintain your site at your Styld subdomain.

When your subscription ends, your booking site goes offline — clients will see an offline message. Your subdomain, content, and client data are all kept. Resubscribe and republish in the app to go live again.

No. Styld doesn't take a percentage of what clients pay you. Your subscription covers the app and hosted booking site. Online payments go through Stripe Connect — standard Stripe processing fees apply, but those go to Stripe, not Styld.

Payments

Styld uses Stripe Connect. You connect your Stripe account in the app, set whether clients pay a deposit or full amount online, and funds go directly to your bank on Stripe's payout schedule.

Yes, to accept online payments and deposits. You connect your Stripe account inside the Styld app. If you prefer cash-only, you can skip online payments and collect in person.

Set a deposit amount per service. Clients pay it online when they book. The remaining balance is collected at the appointment however you prefer. You can also record cash deposits manually in the app.
